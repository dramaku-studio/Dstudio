- CP004: deterministic screenplay scene parsing, dialogue hashes, approved character/location DNA, typed per-shot DAG and capability-pinned prompt snapshots.
- CP005: technical QA evidence, critical identity/audio flags, scoped revision proposals, approved version manifest and explicit human-exception requirement.
